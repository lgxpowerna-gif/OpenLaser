# Power Adjustment & Engraving Features

## Power Adjust

A **Power Adjust** field (±50%) is available on raster operations.
Use it to fine-tune power without changing the base Laser Power value.

## Engraving Quality Presets

A dropdown on each operation applies common engraving settings:

| Preset | Beam | Shades | Overscan | Dither |
|--------|------|--------|----------|--------|
| Fine Detail | 0.1 mm | 16 | 2 mm | Yes |
| Standard | 0.2 mm | 8 | 1 mm | No |
| Fast Draft | 0.3 mm | 4 | 0 mm | No |

## How to use

1. Create a Laser Raster operation.
2. Choose a **Material Preset** (sets power and speed).
3. Choose an **Engraving Quality** preset.
4. Optionally tweak **Power Adjust**.
5. Generate (travel optimization runs automatically).

These are starting points. Test on scrap and adjust.
