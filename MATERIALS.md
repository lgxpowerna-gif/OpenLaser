# Starter Material Profiles (Diode Lasers)

Quick starting points for common materials on 5–10 W diode lasers.
Adjust power and speed for your exact machine and material.

## Plywood (3 mm)
- Power: 60–80%
- Speed: 800–1200 mm/min
- Passes: 1–2
- Notes: Masking tape helps reduce charring.

## Acrylic (3 mm, cast)
- Power: 40–60%
- Speed: 600–900 mm/min
- Passes: 1
- Notes: Use air assist if available. Cast acrylic cuts cleaner than extruded.

## Cardboard / Chipboard
- Power: 20–40%
- Speed: 1500–2500 mm/min
- Passes: 1
- Notes: Watch for fire. Do not leave unattended.

## Leather (vegetable-tanned)
- Power: 30–50%
- Speed: 800–1200 mm/min
- Passes: 1
- Notes: Test on scrap first. Avoid chrome-tanned leather.

## Baltic Birch (3 mm)
- Power: 70–90%
- Speed: 600–1000 mm/min
- Passes: 1–2
- Notes: Good for detailed cuts. Masking recommended.

## How to use
1. Create a new operation in OpenLaser.
2. Set power and feed rate from the ranges above.
3. Run a small test square.
4. Adjust in small steps.

These are starting points only. Always test on scrap.
