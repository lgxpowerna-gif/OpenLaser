# Starter Material Profiles (Diode Lasers)

Practical starting points for 5–10 W diode lasers.
Always test on scrap and adjust in small steps.

## Available in the UI

A **Material Preset** dropdown is available on each operation.
Selecting a preset fills in Laser Power and Cut Rate.

Presets:
- Plywood 3mm (70% @ 1000 mm/min)
- Acrylic 3mm (50% @ 750 mm/min)
- Cardboard (30% @ 2000 mm/min)
- Leather (40% @ 1000 mm/min)
- Baltic Birch 3mm (80% @ 800 mm/min)
- MDF 3mm (60% @ 900 mm/min)

## Manual reference

### Plywood (3 mm)
- Power: 60–80%
- Speed: 800–1200 mm/min
- Passes: 1–2
- Notes: Masking tape reduces charring.

### Acrylic (3 mm, cast)
- Power: 40–60%
- Speed: 600–900 mm/min
- Passes: 1
- Notes: Cast acrylic cuts cleaner. Air assist helps.

### Cardboard / Chipboard
- Power: 20–40%
- Speed: 1500–2500 mm/min
- Passes: 1
- Notes: Fire risk. Do not leave unattended.

### Leather (vegetable-tanned)
- Power: 30–50%
- Speed: 800–1200 mm/min
- Passes: 1
- Notes: Avoid chrome-tanned leather.

### Baltic Birch (3 mm)
- Power: 70–90%
- Speed: 600–1000 mm/min
- Passes: 1–2
- Notes: Good for detailed work. Masking recommended.

### MDF (3 mm)
- Power: 50–70%
- Speed: 700–1100 mm/min
- Passes: 1–2
- Notes: More smoke. Use good ventilation.

## How to use
1. Select an operation.
2. Choose a preset from the **Material Preset** dropdown.
3. Generate (travel optimization runs automatically).
4. Run a small test and adjust by ~10% if needed.
